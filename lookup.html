保健食品實證速查Supplement evidence lookup

同一種保健食品，用在不同目的、不同族群，證據可能完全不同。這裡替每一項「宣稱功效」分別標示證據等級。The same supplement can have very different evidence for different purposes and people. Each claim is graded separately.

等級評的是「某個做法，用在某個目的上」的證據，不是評產品好不好。「健康食品」小綠人標章代表通過特定功效評估，不等於能預防疾病。A grade rates the evidence for one use, not the product. Taiwan’s “health food” mark means a specific claim was reviewed; it does not mean the product prevents disease.
