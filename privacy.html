Privacy

隱私權政策Privacy policy

我們盡量少蒐集資料。你在本站輸入的健康紀錄只存在你自己的裝置；我們只用 Google Analytics 統計匿名的瀏覽數據。We collect as little as possible. Health information you enter stays on your own device; we only use Google Analytics for anonymous visit statistics.

最後更新 2026-09-27Last updated 2026-09-27

01本站是誰經營Who runs this site

實證補給 Evidence for Living 是獨立經營的健康教育網站，與任何醫療機構無關。本站不需要註冊或登入，也沒有會員帳號。Evidence for Living (實證補給) is an independent health-education website, not affiliated with any healthcare institution. There are no accounts, sign-ups or logins.

02你輸入的健康紀錄Health information you enter

本站的工具會用到你輸入的資料，但這些資料只儲存在你目前使用的瀏覽器（localStorage），不會傳送到我們或任何伺服器，我們也看不到：Our tools use what you type, but it is stored only in your current browser (localStorage). It is never sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it:

  • 21 天計畫：每日勾選、體重、血壓、睡眠與活動紀錄。21-Day Reset: daily ticks and weight, blood pressure, sleep and activity entries.
  • 體重管理小幫手：身高、體重、腰圍、體重紀錄、藥物與劑量紀錄、每週習慣。Weight companion: height, weight, waist, weight log, medicine and dose log, weekly habits.
  • 看診準備單：問題、藥物與保健食品清單、想告訴醫師的事。Visit prep sheet: questions, medicine and supplement list, notes for your doctor.
  • 營養計算機：只在畫面上計算，不會儲存。Nutrition calculator: calculated on screen only, not stored.
  • 語言偏好（中文或英文）。Your language preference (Chinese or English).

刪除方式：使用各工具中的「清除」按鈕，或在瀏覽器設定中清除本站的網站資料。使用公用或共用的電腦時，完成後請記得清除。換一台裝置或瀏覽器，紀錄不會跟著過去。To delete: use the Clear button in each tool, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. On a shared computer, clear it when you finish. Records do not move between devices or browsers.

列印或存成 PDF、下載行事曆提醒檔案，也都是在你的裝置上產生，不會經過我們。Printing, saving as PDF and downloading calendar reminders all happen on your device and never pass through us.

03匿名瀏覽統計（Google Analytics）Anonymous visit statistics (Google Analytics)

為了了解哪些內容對讀者有幫助，本站使用 Google Analytics 4。它會透過 Cookie 蒐集一般的瀏覽資訊，例如：瀏覽了哪些頁面、停留時間、從哪個網站連過來、大略的地區（國家或城市），以及裝置與瀏覽器類型。To understand which content helps readers, we use Google Analytics 4. It uses cookies to collect general browsing information such as pages viewed, time on page, the referring site, approximate location (country or city), and device and browser type.

  • 你在工具中輸入的健康紀錄不會傳送給 Google Analytics。Health information you enter in the tools is not sent to Google Analytics.
  • 我們已關閉 Google 信號（跨裝置與人口統計資料）及廣告個人化功能；本站沒有廣告。We have turned off Google signals (cross-device and demographic data) and ad personalisation. This site shows no ads.
  • 依 Google 說明，Google Analytics 4 不會記錄或儲存 IP 位址。According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • 例外：「保健食品實證速查」的搜尋字詞會出現在網址中（例如 ?q=魚油），因此可能出現在瀏覽統計的頁面網址裡。網站頂端的站內搜尋則不會。One exception: search words in the Supplement evidence lookup appear in the page address (for example ?q=fish oil), so they may appear in page addresses in the statistics. The site search box at the top does not.

如果不想被統計，可以安裝 Google 提供的 Google Analytics 停用瀏覽器外掛程式，或在瀏覽器中封鎖 Cookie；網站功能不受影響。Google 如何處理資料，請見 Google 隱私權政策。To opt out, install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or block cookies in your browser; the site still works. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

04其他第三方服務Other third-party services

  • 網站主機：本站由 GitHub Pages 提供，GitHub 可能為了安全與維運記錄連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）。請見 GitHub 隱私權聲明。Hosting: this site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log connection information (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • 字型：本站的字型由 Google Fonts 提供，載入時你的瀏覽器會連線到 Google 的伺服器。Fonts: our fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers to load them.
  • 外部連結：文章中連到其他網站（例如政府機關、期刊）時，會適用該網站的隱私權政策。External links: when you follow a link to another site (such as a government agency or journal), that site’s privacy policy applies.

05我們不做的事What we do not do

  • 不要求註冊，不蒐集姓名、電話、身分證字號或病歷。No registration; we do not collect names, phone numbers, ID numbers or medical records.
  • 不販售、出租或分享任何資料給廣告商。We do not sell, rent or share any data with advertisers.
  • 不提供個人化的醫療判斷；本站內容僅供健康教育參考。We do not provide personal medical judgements; content is for health education only.

06政策更新Changes to this policy

如果本站的資料處理方式改變（例如加入會員或留言功能），我們會先更新本頁，並修改上方的「最後更新」日期。If the way we handle data changes (for example, adding accounts or comments), we will update this page first and change the “last updated” date above.

!

本站提供健康教育，不是診斷或治療。This site offers health education, not diagnosis or treatment.

請不要因為本站內容自行開始、停止或調整任何藥物。如果出現胸痛、嚴重呼吸困難、昏厥或其他緊急症狀，請立即撥打 119 或前往急診。若你有慢性病、懷孕，或曾被醫療人員限制活動，請依照自己的醫療計畫。 Do not start, stop, or change any medicine because of this site. If you have chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, or another emergency symptom, call your local emergency number (119 in Taiwan) or go to an emergency department now. If you have a chronic condition, are pregnant, or have been told to limit activity, follow your own clinical plan.
