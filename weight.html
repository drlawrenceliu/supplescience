1BMI 與腰圍BMI & waist

生理性別Sex

腰圍量法：站立、雙腳與肩同寬，在吐氣結束時，以皮尺繞過肋骨下緣與骨盆上緣（腸骨嵴）的中間點量一圈。Measuring waist: stand with feet shoulder-width apart and, at the end of a normal breath out, measure midway between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone.
